This is an English translation of the Turkish privacy policy and KVKK notice; if they differ, the Turkish text applies.

In short

  • Mira has no required account or membership, no ads and no tracking (analytics).
  • Your conversations don't leave your phone: the AI tutor runs inside your phone (Apple Intelligence on iPhone, Gemini Nano on Android).
  • You don't need an account. If you don't sign in, we don't collect, store or share any data about you.
  • If you want, you can sign in with Apple or Google to back up your progress (see "Optional account and backup").

Optional account and backup

If you use "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google" in Settings:

  • Account data: the account ID and email address Apple or Google sends us (if you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, Apple's private relay address). We don't ask for your name, photo or contacts.
  • Backup: level and app settings, session history (date, topic, time, score, word/speaking measures), mistakes list (corrected sentences), word cards, picture scenes you have seen.
  • Not backed up: your conversations, your voice, Mira's memory notes and device-specific settings.
  • Where: Supabase (data centre in Frankfurt, Germany; EU). Each person can read and write only their own backup.
  • Purpose: only to keep your progress and move it to a new phone. It is not used for ads, analytics or any other purpose and is not shared with anyone.
  • Deletion: Settings > Account > "Delete my account and backup" deletes your account and backup immediately and permanently. Signing out does not delete the backup.
  • Legal basis: KVKK art. 5/2-c (necessary to provide the service) and your request; the backup is kept on a server in the EU (Frankfurt); this transfer only happens when you sign in and choose to back up.

Feedback and problem reports (optional)

The "Feedback / Report a problem" button opens your own email app with a prepared draft; you send the email yourself, and Mira sends nothing in the background. The draft contains only: the text you write, a screenshot if you choose one (downscaled, photo metadata removed), app version, phone model, operating system version, selected language and level. You can see and delete all of it before sending. Your conversations, name, contacts or location are not added.

Emails we receive and your email address are used only to understand and fix the problem and to reply to you; they are not shared and are deleted at the latest 12 months after the issue is closed. To have them deleted earlier, just write to the same address. (KVKK art. 5/2-f: legitimate interest; the email service is Google, whose servers may be outside Türkiye.)

Full conversation texts are not saved; when a session ends, only a summary line (date, topic, minutes, score) remains.

Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: Mira listens only when you press the microphone button or when "Listen to me when Mira stops" is on. If you don't give permission, you can practise by typing.
  • Internet: Mira itself does not send data. Downloading the phone's AI and voice models is done by the operating system.

About the AI

  • The tutors (Mira and Leo) are AI, not real people. Their face images are AI-generated and do not belong to a real person.
  • AI can make mistakes: corrections and translations may not always be right.
  • The app is designed for people aged 13 and over.

Your rights

Under article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information, and to have it corrected or deleted and to object. If you haven't signed in, we hold no record about you; if you have, you can delete your account and backup yourself in the app or ask us to delete them; data on your phone is removed completely by deleting the app. Questions: support2they@gmail.com (you can also use the "Feedback" button in the app).

Transfers abroad: if you don't sign in, Mira makes none. If you sign in, your account data and backup are stored on a server in the EU (Frankfurt) (see "Optional account and backup"). If your device's own speech recognition runs off the device, Apple's or Google's own policy applies.
