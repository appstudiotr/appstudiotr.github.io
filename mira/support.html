Support

Having a problem or have an idea? Write to us at support2they@gmail.com. In the app you can also go to Settings > Support > "Feedback / Report a problem" and attach a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

Which phones does Mira work on?
The AI tutor runs inside your phone: iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and later) or Android phones with Gemini Nano. Apple Intelligence must be turned on in Settings.

Do I need an account?
No. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional; it only backs up your progress and moves it to a new phone.

Can I use the app in English?
Yes. Go to Settings > Language and interface: choose the app language (English or Turkish) and your native language. Corrections, translations, word meanings and the session summary come in your native language.

The microphone doesn't work.
Turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition for Mira in your phone's Settings. You can also type your answers.

How do I delete my account?
Settings > Account > "Delete my account and backup". Your account and backup are deleted immediately and permanently.

Are my conversations sent anywhere?
No, your conversations don't leave your phone. Details: Privacy Policy.
